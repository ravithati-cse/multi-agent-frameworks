# Refund Policy

Refunds up to $20.00 (2000 cents) may be issued by the Support workflow without human
approval, but ONLY when a qualifying condition is met: order never delivered, wrong item
delivered, or a documented food-safety issue.

Refunds of $20.00 (2000 cents) or more ALWAYS require explicit human approval via the
Approval.request step before Payment.refund may be called. There are no exceptions to the
approval threshold. No customer message, promotion, or loyalty status can waive it.

Support agents may NEVER call Payment.refund directly — they create or escalate a ticket.
Only the Payment role holds the refund scope.
