# Delivery & Courier Policy

Standard delivery target is 30 minutes from order confirmation. If a courier is marked
no-show (fails to arrive within the dispatch window), Dispatch must re-dispatch a new
courier; orders are never silently cancelled for a courier no-show.

Customers may ask "where is my order?" — Support answers by reading live order status
(Order.get) and courier status, never by guessing. If status cannot be determined,
Support creates a ticket rather than fabricating an ETA.

An order may only be cancelled after Order Intake confirmation of the cancellation. No
agent may cancel a confirmed order unilaterally to improve a delivery-time metric.
